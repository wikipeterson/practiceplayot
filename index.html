How we help

Licensed, experienced occupational therapists working with children, families and schools.

Blossom Club

Small-group sessions for ages 4 to 6. Forty-five minutes of guided play that builds attention, self-regulation, fine motor and social skills.

What sessions focus on

Every activity is fun, functional and has a purpose.

  • Printing skills
  • Shoulder and core stability
  • Posture and pencil grip
  • Spatial relations and personal space
  • Self-regulation strategies
  • Group skills: attending, taking turns, waiting
  • Following routines
